Prediction: We expect converting numbers to text to take much more time than simple arithmetic operations because formatting a number requires multiple processing steps. We expect float-to-text conversion to be more expensive than integer-to-text conversion. We expect sending a character through Serial at 115200 baud to take much longer than the CPU needs for a single integer addition. We also expect decimal, hexadecimal and binary representations to have different transmission costs because they can require different numbers of characters.
